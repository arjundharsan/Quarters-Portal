The allotment process

Every allotment follows this order.

  1. Circular published

    The Estate section announces the quarters available and the last date to apply.

  2. You apply

    Apply for quarters of your eligible pay level within the circular dates.

  3. Eligibility check Estate section

    We confirm you applied for your eligible pay level. If you already occupy quarters, you may seek a change only after 3 years, counted as on the circular date. Otherwise you are not eligible.

  4. Seniority requested

    The eligible applicant list goes to the Establishment section to obtain seniority.

  5. Final list published

    Once seniority is received, the final list is published.

  6. Allotment prepared and approved Director

    Quarters are matched by seniority and eligibility, then sent to the Director for approval.

  7. Declaration form

    Allottees submit a signed declaration form. Print the form.

  8. Allotment order issued

    You receive your allotment order.

  9. Sign the registers

    Sign the occupation register for the new quarters. If you are changing quarters, also sign the vacation register of the quarters you are leaving.

Questions

Can I apply for a higher type than my pay level?

No. Applications for any type other than your eligible pay level are rejected at the eligibility check.

I already have quarters. When can I apply for a change?

After 3 years in your current quarters, counted as on the date of the circular.

What do I sign after receiving the allotment order?

The occupation register. For a change of quarters, also the vacation register of your previous quarters.

Who decides the seniority?

The Establishment section provides it. The Director approves the final allotment.
