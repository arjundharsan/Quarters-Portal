Official allotment workflow

Stages follow the traditional EMD procedure you described. All records here are fictional.

The system tracks progress and records decisions; it does not automatically determine official seniority or make final allotment decisions. Those must follow approved NITT rules and competent-authority approval.

Workflow stages

  1. Publish circular — record circular number, publication date, application deadline and eligibility cutoff date.
  2. Receive applications — register each application and requested quarters category.
  3. Eligibility scrutiny — verify pay-level eligibility and, for current occupants, the three-year change-of-quarters rule as on the circular cutoff date. Record reasons for ineligibility.
  4. Forward eligible list to Establishment — record forwarding date and reference number.
  5. Record seniority received — enter the official seniority particulars/reference received from Establishment.
  6. Publish final eligible list — record publication date and list reference.
  7. Submit proposed allotment to Director — prepare the proposal based on approved seniority, eligibility and available quarters; record approval status and reference.
  8. Collect declaration — record receipt of the required declaration form.
  9. Issue allotment order — record order number and date after approval and required formalities.
  10. Register handover — record new-quarter occupation register entry and, for a change of quarters, previous-quarter vacation register entry.

After eligibility scrutiny

  1. Review each result and correct missing or inaccurate data.
  2. Apply the approved pay-level/category matrix and all other applicable rules.
  3. Have authorized staff approve the eligible and ineligible lists.
  4. Forward only the approved eligible list to Establishment for official seniority particulars.
  5. Record the Establishment communication reference and seniority details when received; do not calculate or invent seniority in EMD.

Import and export

Prepare the inventory in Excel, then save it as CSV UTF-8 for this prototype.

Import quarters CSV

Required columns: quarter,category,zone,status,occupant. Column names are case-insensitive. Status should be Vacant, Occupied, or Maintenance.

Excel workbooks (.xlsx) are not read directly in this prototype. Open the relevant sheet in Excel and use Save As → CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited).

Data handling notes

  • Export creates a local CSV download.
  • Import replaces the current demo inventory.
  • No data is uploaded to a server by this prototype.
  • Do not import real employee details or confidential institute records into this demo.

Suggested Excel columns

quarter, category, zone, status, occupant, occupation_date, vacation_date, remarks
